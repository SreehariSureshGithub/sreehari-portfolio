## How was Sreehari's portfolio website designed and built (the site's design, UI/UX, layout and front end)?
UI/UX design isn't Sreehari's background, so the website's UI/UX design and front end were implemented with AI tools: the first version on Lovable, then he moved it to VS Code and continued with Claude Code. What was his were the design decisions: the colour palette, the layout, the typography, removing decorative effects, and presenting his projects honestly from his GitHub. The AI tools implemented those decisions; he did not design the UI/UX himself. This covers the website's design and front end only.
