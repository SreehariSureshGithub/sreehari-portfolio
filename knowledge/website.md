## How was Sreehari's portfolio website designed and built (the site's design, UI/UX, layout and front end)?
UI/UX design isn't Sreehari's background, so he built his portfolio website's design and front end with AI tools: the first version on Lovable, then he moved it to VS Code and continued with Claude Code. The design decisions were his: the colour palette, the layout, the typography, removing decorative effects, and presenting his projects honestly from his GitHub. The AI tools implemented those decisions. This covers the website's design and front end only.
