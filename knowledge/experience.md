### Car dealership – VIN reconciliation pipeline
- Built a Python reconciliation pipeline for a car dealership that matches the dealer's stock sheet against QuickBooks report exports and bank statements, per VIN and per deal cycle.
- Deal cycles are handled separately, so a car bought back and resold under the same VIN is reconciled cycle by cycle.
- Every link to a QuickBooks bill or invoice is labelled with how it was made (document reference, VIN, or VIN plus amount), and ambiguous cases go to a review queue instead of being guessed.
- It calculates consignment settlements and writes one Excel workbook with summary, reconciliation, settlement, discrepancy and review sheets.
- On the real dealership dataset it was built from (about 4,000 deal cycles), it matched the hand-verified results on 99.5% of purchase references and 100% of purchase values.
- Code: https://github.com/SreehariSureshGithub/vin-reconciliation-pipeline

## Sreehari Suresh – AI Intern at Campervan Factory Pvt. Ltd., Chennai (Nov 2024 – Jul 2025)
At Campervan Factory, Sreehari designed a hybrid LiDAR + camera obstacle warning system for caravan safety, built as a Raspberry Pi prototype.
Tools used in this role: Raspberry Pi, LiDAR, camera modules, Python, OpenCV.
- The system separates real hazards from false alarms, such as leaves, that ultrasonic sensors alone would trigger on.

## Sreehari Suresh – AI Intern at ISMO Bio-Photonics Pvt. Ltd., Chennai (Aug 2024 – Nov 2024)
At ISMO Bio-Photonics, Sreehari worked on a real-time particle counting system that uses line-scan camera imagery.

- Optimised the model for edge deployment on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano, cutting median inference latency from 14 ms to 4 ms (71% faster) through ONNX export and TensorRT acceleration.
- The optimised prototype was used for an investor demo.
